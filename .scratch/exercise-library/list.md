# Exercise library: proposed list

Status: draft for John's review (2026-09-27). Nothing built.

Written for Coar in Coar's own terms. It's grouped by primary muscle group, and inside each
group by equipment. "Also works" lists the secondary groups, most important first. Names are
singular and in sentence case, like the rest of Coar. Anything with a detail you always use
(an angle, a grip) can be renamed once it's added.

Muscle groups: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Forearms, Core, Quads, Hamstrings,
Glutes, Calves (Other exists in Coar, and nothing here uses it). Equipment: Barbell, Dumbbell, Cable, Machine, Smith machine, EZ bar,
Kettlebell, Band, Bodyweight, or Other (named).
